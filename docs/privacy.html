Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Summary

Time Limiter for Instagram does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything it stores stays on your device.

What is stored, and where

  • How much time you spent on Instagram and X today, and when the current session started.
  • Your settings: daily limit, curfew time, and whether the extensions page is locked.
  • A salted PBKDF2-SHA-256 hash of your password. The password itself is never stored and can't be read back from the hash.

All of this lives only in your browser's local extension storage. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else. Removing the extension deletes it.

Permissions and why they're needed

  • Tabs: to tell whether the active tab is Instagram or X, and to close or replace those tabs when a limit is reached.
  • Access to instagram.com and x.com (and twitter.com): to show the 10-second countdown on the page before it closes. The extension doesn't read or change anything else on these sites.
  • Scripting: to display that countdown.
  • Storage: to save your usage time and settings on your device.
  • Alarms: to check time limits on schedule.

No tracking

The extension has no analytics, ads or remote code, and makes no network requests.
